# Give Wagami A an original field-tablet composition

Wagami A retains a device-like monitor feel but uses a matte-charcoal rugged field-tablet shell with
asymmetric corners and an almost edge-to-edge screen, rather than reusing Wagami X or Wagami Z's
rounded blue housing. Its top vital-card strip, dominant waveform workspace, separate defibrillation
state, visible urgent touch actions, and six permanent right-side touchscreen task launchers
above compact defibrillation status reject both a generic
software-only dashboard and the existing models' button-bank or touch-ribbon compositions. This
choice preserves tactile training through physical Power, Charge, and Shock controls while making
the overall visual and interaction structure independently recognizable.

The six launchers are 12-lead, EtCO2, Medications, Call Info, Print/Capture, and Configure.
Call Info leads to the trainee Assignment dashboard; the medication Event Log remains within
Medications, not as an Events launcher. First-pass and v2 concept images predate this destination
correction and are not a functional specification.
