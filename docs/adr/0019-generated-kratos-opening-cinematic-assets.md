# Generate Kratos artwork and use product-owner-supplied cinematic audio

The public landing-page Opening cinematic uses newly generated layered artwork based on supplied
Kratos, Blades of Chaos, and choreography references together with the two audio recordings supplied
by the product owner. The application does not copy the reference images or video into its finished
assets; the product owner accepts responsibility for confirming public-use rights to the recognizable
third-party character and supplied recordings. This fidelity-first choice is recorded because an
original non-franchise chained-blade warrior would materially reduce intellectual-property risk but
would not meet the approved creative direction.

## Consequences

- Generated wind-up and apex artwork requires explicit product-owner approval before animation code.
- Source audio remains intact; implementation may create a shorter derived grunt synchronized to the
  strike while preserving the full spoken-title recording.
- No extracted game footage, models, logos, music, or sound effects may enter the application.
- If public-use rights cannot be confirmed, the cinematic must be replaced with an original non-
  franchise warrior and voice treatment before public deployment.
