# Distinguish Wagami A advised and manual charging

Wagami A automatically begins its timed charge when Analyze identifies a captured shockable rhythm,
while one physical Charge press begins a manual charge without shock advice. This deliberately
replaces A's former shared two-step “advice, then press Charge” workflow: automatic charging is a
device consequence recorded by the Analyze result rather than a trainee Charge action, whereas a
manual Charge press remains attributable to the trainee. X and Z keep their existing defibrillation
policies, and both A charge origins retain distinct post-shock outcomes.
