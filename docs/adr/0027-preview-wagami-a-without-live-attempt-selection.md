# Preview Wagami A without exposing it in live Attempts

During its phased construction, Wagami A is available from the base-link `/?dev=3` shortcut as a
powered-on, dispatch-bypassing device preview visibly labeled `PREVIEW`, but it is not selectable
for Instructor-led Attempts or Room join until its controls, reports, and Spectator path are ready.
This keeps design review easy while avoiding partly implemented training workflows; adding a
direct preview path is deliberately different from enabling the model in production scenarios.
