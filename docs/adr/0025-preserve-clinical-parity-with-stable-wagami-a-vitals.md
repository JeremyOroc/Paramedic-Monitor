# Preserve clinical parity while keeping Wagami A vitals stable

Wagami A changes how trainees reach clinical functions but preserves Wagami X's working boot,
defibrillation, PNI, EtCO2, 12-lead, and CPR timing/state rules and scenario outcomes. Unlike X,
its top vital cards do not relocate when analysis or charging begins; a dedicated panel changes to
present defibrillation progress and feedback. This trades literal device-workflow mimicry for a
stable clinical reading area while avoiding an unreviewed change to simulation behavior.
