# Keep Wagami A's shell across secondary views

Every Wagami A destination, including 12-lead and Call Info, replaces content inside the
device's inner display rather than replacing the whole device with a shell-free browser page.
The outer shell and its guarded physical-style controls remain visible and operable so active
clinical processes and essential device actions remain coherent across navigation; in A,
`full-display` means the entire inner screen, not the entire browser viewport. Wagami X and Z
retain their existing presentation behavior.
